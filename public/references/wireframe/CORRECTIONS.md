# Wireframe Corrections

The three wireframe PNGs in this directory (`renuvol-wireframe-01.png`,
`-02.png`, `-03.png`) are static images and are not edited in place.
Approved deviations from what they show are recorded here instead. Treat
this file as authoritative over the PNGs wherever they conflict.

## Section 04 — formula section: 5 components, not 4

`renuvol-wireframe-01.png` shows Section 04 ("«ВНУТРИ RENUVOL» / ИНТЕЛЛЕКТ
ФОРМУЛЫ") with **4** interactive hotspot cards (Компонент 01–04) around the
central product.

**Correction:** the section now has **5** components — PDO, PN (полинуклеотид),
Vitamin C, Glutathione, Hyaluronate (sodium hyaluronate) — matching how
RENUVOL's formula is consistently presented across
`public/references/instagram/01-formula-all-in-one/` and the related
ingredient-specific categories (`17-vitamin-c/`, `18-hyaluronate/`,
`19-glutathione/`).

See `docs/design-system.md` § Section 04 for the full content model and
geometry direction (an asymmetric/orbital 5-node system, not 5 identical
cards mechanically substituted into the old 4-card grid), and
`docs/claims-verification.md` for how each ingredient's claims are gated.

## Illustrative copy in the wireframes is not verified content

Beyond the component-count correction above, several other pieces of text
baked into the wireframe mockups are placeholder/illustrative and must not
be treated as approved copy — see `docs/asset-audit.md` § Wireframes and
`docs/claims-verification.md` for the full list (the manifesto's 93%
statistic, Section 08's protocol intervals, Section 10's named certificates
and manufacturer figures, Section 11's three illustrative cases, and the
placeholder footer address). This file covers only the Section 04 structural
correction; the content-accuracy caveats live in the documents linked above.
