# RENUVOL Instagram Reference Library — Batch 1

Use this folder as source/reference material for Claude Code.

## Source-of-truth rule
Instagram content may be used to understand product positioning and visual language, but marketing claims are not automatically verified medical facts.

Cross-check claims against `/content/product-facts.md`, `/content/certificates.md`, manufacturer documents and clinical sources.

## Visual language observed
- icy / pearl light blue
- cobalt blue accents
- silver / chrome
- glass, transparency and luminous rings
- molecular spheres
- clean clinical environments
- dark navy for technology/credibility scenes
- orange as an INCUBE/LABINCUBE accent

GrowWall remains the experiential reference. The approved RENUVOL wireframe remains the structural blueprint.

## Claims that require verification
- Nobel / “Nobel technology” linkage
- exact clinical percentages
- PDO vs PLLA comparison
- safety and biodegradation timing
- patents / awards / global reach
- manufacturer history
- certificates and regulatory claims
